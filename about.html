About ELVION

One job: the right bulb for your instrument.

ELVION is an independent brand that sells replacement bulbs for diagnostic otoscopes and ophthalmoscopes.

What we do

A small range, clearly labelled.

We offer six lamp references. For each one, a model guide shows the voltage and lamp type we have checked, so you can compare them with your instrument's manual.

Orders go through our Amazon storefront. Amazon takes payment and handles shipping and tracking.

If you are not sure which bulb you need, send us the model number. Our team reads and answers every message.

How we work

What you can expect

Clear information

We only publish details we can check against our listings and published lamp references. If something is not confirmed, we say so instead of guessing.

Help before you buy

Send us the instrument or lamp reference by form, email, phone or WhatsApp and we will help you match it.

Honest branding

ELVION bulbs are compatible replacements. Instrument brand names appear on our site only to describe compatibility. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by those companies.

Not sure which bulb you need?

Send us the model number printed on your instrument. We will tell you which ELVION bulb matches before you buy.
