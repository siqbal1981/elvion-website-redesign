Our focus

Precision, durability, and clarity — for equipment that can't afford to underperform.

ELVION exists to solve one specific problem well: keeping diagnostic instruments properly lit. Every bulb we supply is built to match the exact voltage and fitment of the device it replaces, so exam-room lighting is never the variable a clinician has to worry about.

We work across the device families clinics already use — Welch Allyn® — rather than forcing practices to change equipment to get reliable replacement parts.
