Welch Allyn ophthalmoscope bulb replacement: find the lamp reference by model

Bulbs are sold by lamp reference, not by instrument model. This page matches Welch Allyn ophthalmoscope model numbers to the lamp reference Welch Allyn's own chart lists for them.

If you search for a Welch Allyn ophthalmoscope bulb replacement, you usually start from the number on your instrument, such as 11820 or 12810. The bulb itself is identified by a lamp reference, such as 03800-U or 03900-U. Welch Allyn's own lamp replacement chart connects the two. The table below repeats the ophthalmoscope entries from that chart for the lamp references ELVION sells.

Check the voltage before you order

03800-U and 03900-U are listed at different voltages. The lamp voltage has to match the instrument's supply voltage, so read the bulb voltage guide and your manual first.

If your ophthalmoscope model is not listed

The table covers only the lamp references ELVION sells. A model that is not listed may use a different reference. Check your manual, or send us your instrument model and we will tell you whether we can confirm a matching ELVION bulb. We do not guess.

Before you order

  1. Confirm the lamp reference in your instrument manual matches the one in the table.
  2. Check the lamp voltage against your handle.
  3. Open the ELVION page for that reference and use its Buy button to see the exact Amazon listing.
  4. When the bulb arrives, follow how to replace a bulb safely. Light still not working? See what to check when the light will not come on.

Frequently asked questions

Which lamp reference does Welch Allyn list for PanOptic ophthalmoscopes?

Welch Allyn's lamp replacement chart lists lamp reference 03800-U for PanOptic ophthalmoscopes 11800, 11810 and 11820. Confirm it in your own manual before you order.

Which lamp reference does Welch Allyn list for PocketScope ophthalmoscopes?

Welch Allyn's lamp replacement chart lists lamp reference 03900-U for PocketScope ophthalmoscopes 11110, 12810 and 13000. Confirm it in your own manual before you order.

Is the 03800-U bulb the same as the 03900-U bulb?

No. They are separate lamp references that the chart lists for different instruments, and ELVION lists them at different voltages (3.5 V and 2.5 V). Match the reference in your manual. See the bulb voltage guide.

My ophthalmoscope model is not in the table. What should I do?

The table only covers the references ELVION sells. A model that is not listed may use a different lamp reference. Check your manual or the full Welch Allyn chart, or send us the model printed on your instrument and we will tell you whether we can confirm a matching ELVION bulb.
