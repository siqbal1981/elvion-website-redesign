## Competitors reviewed (public pages, 2026-10-11)
- **OakTree Products (03100 page):** minimal copy, no H2s, FAQs, schema or reviews. States a voltage and a list of compatible heads. Price is hidden behind a login. Offers a bulb-change troubleshooting PDF.
- **Anthony Products (03100 page):** description names Welch Allyn otoscopes and illuminators; no visible headings or schema; no voltage stated.
- **Lite Source (03800-U page):** Description / Specifications / Related items headings; claims a genuine OEM part with voltage and life figures; lists PanOptic heads; shows a price. No FAQ or schema.
- **Search results** for the model keywords are dominated by distributors and retailers (Amazon, eBay, McKesson, Anthony, OakTree, Lite Source and others). Their voltage claims disagree with each other, so none are copied to ELVION.
- Amazon, anthonyproducts.com search and litesourceinc.com home could not be fetched (robots), so ELVION's own Amazon listing wording was not compared.

## Gaps ELVION can fill
- Competitors have thin or no FAQs, reference-reading explanations, how-to guides or schema. ELVION model pages already carry these.
- No competitor page seen has a "light will not come on" checklist or a "how to find the lamp reference" guide. Both are added in this change.

## Rules kept
- No voltage, wattage, compatibility claims, prices, stock or reviews are published without a verified source. A competitor's specification does not prove ELVION's bulb has the same one.
- Compatibility statements rely on the manufacturer lamp chart and manuals cited on the pages.
