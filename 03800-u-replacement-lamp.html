Which instruments it fits

The 03800-U lamp reference is listed for Welch Allyn® PanOptic ophthalmoscopes 11800, 11810 and 11820. Our Amazon listing and published lamp-reference listings agree on this.

Check it matches before you order

  • Find the lamp reference in your instrument's manual. It should read 03800-U.
  • Check the voltage in the manual matches 3.5V.
  • Do not choose by shape alone. Bulbs that look alike are not always interchangeable.
  • Not sure? Send us your instrument model and we will check it.

Replacing the lamp

Switch the instrument off and let the old lamp cool before you touch it. Follow the instructions that came with your instrument.

03800-U questions

What voltage is the ELVION 03800-U bulb?

The ELVION 03800-U is a 3.5V halogen lamp. Check that your instrument's manual also lists 3.5V for the lamp before ordering.

Which instruments use the 03800-U lamp?

The 03800-U lamp reference is listed for Welch Allyn® PanOptic ophthalmoscopes 11800, 11810 and 11820. Always confirm the lamp reference in your own instrument's manual, because instruments that look alike can take different lamps.
