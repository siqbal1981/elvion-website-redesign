Choosing the right otoscope bulb

Bulbs that look alike are not always interchangeable. Look up the lamp reference in your instrument manual or on the old bulb's packaging, check that the voltage matches your handle, and only then choose a model. Our guide How to choose a replacement otoscope or ophthalmoscope bulb walks through each check.

When you are ready to swap the bulb, read how to replace an otoscope or ophthalmoscope bulb safely and follow the instructions for your own instrument.

Looking for ophthalmoscope bulbs instead? See replacement ophthalmoscope bulbs. The lamp references 03000-U and 03100-U are also sold by ELVION: see the 03000-U and 03100-U model guides, or the shop page for all six models.
