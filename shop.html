Before you buy

Match the number, not the look.

Diagnostic bulbs that look almost the same can have a different voltage or base. A bulb that fits in the socket is not always the right bulb. The lamp reference printed in your instrument's manual is the safest way to choose.

ELVION bulbs are compatible replacements. They are not original manufacturer parts, and ELVION is not affiliated with or endorsed by any instrument manufacturer.

Not sure which bulb you need?

Send us the model number printed on your instrument. We will tell you which ELVION bulb matches before you buy.
