How to order

Three steps to the right bulb

1

Find the number

Look for the lamp reference in your instrument's manual or on the old bulb's packaging. The model number of the instrument head also works.

2

Match the model

Pick the same reference on our shop page and check its model guide. Bulbs that look alike are not interchangeable.

3

Order on Amazon

The Buy button opens the exact Amazon listing for that model. Amazon handles payment, shipping and tracking.
