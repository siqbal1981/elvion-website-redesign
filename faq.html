Choosing a bulb

How do I know which bulb fits my instrument?

Find the lamp reference in your instrument's manual (for example 03100-U) and choose the same reference on our shop page. If you cannot find it, send us the make and model printed on your instrument and we will help. You can use the contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp.

Which models does ELVION offer?

Six lamp references: 03000-U, 03100-U, 03400-U, 03800-U, 03900-U and HPX06500. All six can be ordered on Amazon from our shop page.

Can I use a bulb that looks the same?

Not safely. Bulbs that look alike can have a different voltage or base. Always match the lamp reference, not the shape.

Specifications

What voltage are ELVION bulbs?

It depends on the model. Our current Amazon listings show 3.5V for 03000-U, 03100-U, 03400-U, 03800-U and HPX06500, and 2.5V for 03900-U. The voltage for each model is on the shop page. Check it against your instrument's manual before ordering.

What type of bulb are they?

Our current Amazon listings describe all six models (03000-U, 03100-U, 03400-U, 03800-U, 03900-U and HPX06500) as halogen lamps.

How many bulbs come in a pack?

Most listings are for a single bulb. 03100-U is also sold in a 2-pack and a 5-pack. The pack size is shown in each Amazon listing title.

Do you publish bulb life or brightness figures?

Not at the moment. We do not publish rated life, lumens or colour temperature. Contact us if you need these for a purchasing form.

Brand and ordering

Is ELVION made by or connected to the instrument manufacturer?

No. ELVION is an independent brand. Instrument brand names are trademarks of their owners and are used on this site only to describe compatibility. ELVION is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by them.

Where do I buy?

On Amazon. Each Buy button on our shop page opens the exact listing for that model, where you can see the current price and delivery time.

Can I order a larger quantity for a clinic?

Yes. Contact us with the model and quantity and we will help with the order.

Not sure which bulb you need?

Send us the model number printed on your instrument. We will tell you which ELVION bulb matches before you buy.
