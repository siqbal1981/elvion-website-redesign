Check it matches your instrument

  • Find the lamp reference in your instrument's manual. It should read 03100-U.
  • Check that the manual lists 3.5V for the lamp.
  • Do not choose by shape alone. Bulbs that look alike are not always interchangeable.
  • Not sure? Send us your instrument model and we will check it.

Replacing the lamp

Switch the instrument off and let the old lamp cool before you touch it. Follow the instructions that came with your instrument.
