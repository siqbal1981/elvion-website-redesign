Model 03100-U

Check the reference before ordering

The 03100-U reference is listed by the original instrument manufacturer as a 3.5V Halogen HPX replacement lamp. It is associated with selected diagnostic otoscopes, transilluminators, illuminators, and related instrument heads.

Compatibility guidance

Manufacturer-listed instrument references

Manufacturer documentation lists the 03100-U lamp for selected models including otoscopes 20000, 20200, 20202, 21700, 25000, 25020; transilluminators 26530, 26538, 27000, 27050, 41100, 43300; tongue blade holder 28100; and handle adapter 73500.

Confirm the exact model printed on your instrument. Physical resemblance alone does not establish compatibility.

Before replacing the lamp

Disconnect the instrument from power and allow the old lamp to cool before handling it. Follow the instructions supplied with the instrument. If the reference or voltage is unclear, contact ELVION before ordering.
